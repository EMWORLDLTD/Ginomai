A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Get to know your next Sunday sidekick.

Who is Ginomia built for?

Ginomia is built for church media teams, worship leaders, and volunteers who manage scripture, lyrics, announcements, and live service presentation.

Can I use it with OBS or vMix?

Yes. Ginomia provides browser-based display outputs that can be added to your OBS or vMix setup. You can manage your sanctuary projection and broadcast presentation from the same studio.

Can another volunteer help remotely?

Yes. Start remote control from Broadcast Hub and share the operator link and pairing code. A volunteer on the same local network can use a phone, tablet, or browser, with the permissions you give them.

Do I have to project every slide immediately?

You choose. Instant click-to-project is the default, and you can enable Preview → Take live in Settings. Hold live keeps the current output steady while you prepare what comes next.

Can I prepare and reuse my service plan?

Yes. Save service sessions, create snapshots, and export or import your service plan. Your songs and scripture can be organized before the service begins.
